# Dedication and offering

## Offering moment (P1)

See [session-experience](session-experience.md#the-offering-moment).

## Dedicate a session (P2)

- Dedicate japa to someone or something: a sick relative, departed ancestors (e.g. during Pitru Paksha), a newborn, world peace.
- Dedications appear on the offering screen and in history.
- Optionally let the person know ("Your family chanted 1,008 names for you").

## Digital japa book (P1 → P3)

- [Likhita japa](chanting-modes.md#likhita-japa) entries fill pages of a digital book.
- **P3:** order a **printed book** of your likhita japa, like a Rama Koti notebook.

## Temple offerings (P4)

- Offer japa totals or printed books at partner temples.
- See [partners-and-revenue](partners-and-revenue.md).
