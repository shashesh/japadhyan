# Festival programs

Special naam japam programs for festivals and observances.

## P1 — one program, done well: Navaratri

- 9 days, a form of the Devi each day, her mantra, a daily target.
- Short daily reading on that form of the Devi.
- Offering moment adapted for the Devi.
- Completion certificate / summary at the end.

## P2 — full festival calendar

- Built-in **panchang**, location-aware.
- Programs start automatically when a festival approaches (with a heads-up a few days before):
  - Maha Shivratri, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Ram Navami, Hanuman Jayanti, Diwali, Kartik month, Shravan month, Guru Purnima, Pitru Paksha
- Recurring observances: **Ekadashi** and **Pradosh** reminders.
- Brahma muhurta alarm from local sunrise.

## P3 — together

- **Live worldwide counter** for major festivals (e.g. everyone's Shivratri japa adding up).
- Family and community festival goals — see [community](community.md).

## P4 — other faiths

- Ramadan dhikr, Vesak, Gurpurab, Paryushana, Lent and others — see [multi-faith](multi-faith.md).
