# Community

Shared practice without competition ([decision](../../decisions/2026-09-21-collective-not-competitive.md)).

## Family and friends groups

- Create a group, invite by link.
- **Shared goals:** "1 crore Ram Naam as a family for Diwali".
- See the group total and your contribution; individual counts visible only if each member opts in.

## Live worldwide festival counter

- During major festivals, everyone's japa adds to one live total.
- Anonymous by default.

## Group chanting rooms `hard`

- Chant together in real time, like a virtual satsang.
- A leader chants; others follow; the room's total grows.
- Needs moderation and safety design before launch.

## What we will not build

- Public leaderboards or ranking individuals against each other.
- Sharing of [private guru mantras](mantra-library.md#private-guru-mantra-p1) in any form.
