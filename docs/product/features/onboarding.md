# Onboarding

Goal: from install to first repetition in **under one minute**. No account required.

## Flow

1. **"Who or what do you pray to?"** — choose a deity, a tradition, or "just me".
2. **Choose a mantra** from suggestions for that choice, or add your own (with the [private guru mantra](mantra-library.md#private-guru-mantra-p1) option).
3. **Pick your mala** — every [mala style](chanting-modes.md#mala-tap) is open; one is pre-selected from the choice above (e.g. Tulsi for Krishna, Rudraksha for Shiva), so one tap continues.
4. **Set a daily goal** — 1, 3 or 11 malas, or a custom count.
5. **Pick a reminder time** — with suggestions such as Brahma muhurta or dusk, calculated from local sunrise/sunset.
6. **Start chanting** — lands directly on the [session screen](session-experience.md).

Every choice here can be changed later, and nothing is locked ([decision](../../decisions/2026-09-21-free-flow-nothing-locked.md)).

## Later, not up front

- Account creation (for sync) offered after the first few sessions.
- Introduction to other chanting modes offered gradually, one at a time.
- Suggest a [sankalpa](sankalpa-and-progress.md) after a week of practice.
