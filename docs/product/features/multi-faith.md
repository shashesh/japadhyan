# Multi-faith

The long-term goal: a one-stop app for recitation practice in every faith.

## Approach

- **Launch Hindu-first**, deep rather than thin.
- **From P1**, the data model must not assume Hindu concepts: traditions, round sizes (108, 99, 33, 59…), offering styles and calendars are all configurable.
- **An advisor from each tradition** reviews its mantras, content, imagery and wording before release.

## Traditions (P4)

| Tradition | Practice | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buddhist | *Om Mani Padme Hum*, *Nam Myoho Renge Kyo*, *Namo Amituofo* | 108-bead mala common |
| Sikh | *Waheguru* simran | Naam simran central to practice |
| Jain | *Navkar Mantra* | 108 beads |
| Islamic | Tasbih / dhikr: *SubhanAllah*, *Alhamdulillah*, *Allahu Akbar* (33× each) | 33 or 99 beads; no imagery of God |
| Christian | Rosary, the Jesus Prayer | Rosary has decades and specific prayers |

## Sensitivities

- Some traditions avoid depictions of the divine — imagery must be optional and tradition-aware.
- Wording like "offering" should adapt per tradition.
- Festival calendars differ (lunar Hijri, liturgical, Nanakshahi…).
