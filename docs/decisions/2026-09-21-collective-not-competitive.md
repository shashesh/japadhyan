# Collective goals, not leaderboards

## Context

Social features drive engagement, and leaderboards are the common pattern. But ranking devotees against each other feeds ego, which runs against the spirit of japa.

## Decision

Community features use **shared goals** (family targets, worldwide festival counters, group chanting rooms). **No public leaderboards** or individual rankings.

## Consequences

- Engagement comes from belonging and shared purpose rather than competition.
- Individual counts in groups are visible only with each member's consent.
