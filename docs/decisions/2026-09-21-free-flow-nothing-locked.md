# Free flow: nothing is locked

## Context

A common gamification pattern is to unlock things as users progress: new malas after 10,000 chants, new sounds after a 30-day streak. For a devotee, a mala is part of their practice, not a reward. A Vaishnava chants on Tulsi and a Shaiva on Rudraksha from their first day. Making someone earn the tools of their own tradition runs against the spirit of japa.

## Decision

Practice in the app is **free flow**: every devotee can use every part of their practice from day one, in the way they choose.

- **Nothing is earned or unlocked** by counts, streaks, milestones or time spent in the app.
- **Mala style is a free choice at setup** (Rudraksha, Tulsi, crystal, sandalwood and others), suggested by the devotee's tradition and changeable at any time.
- **Defaults are suggestions, never rules.** Round size, mode, mantra, imagery and offering style can all be changed.
- **Milestones celebrate; they never unlock.**

## Consequences

- Motivation comes from the practice itself, sankalpas and shared goals, not from rewards.
- No malas, sounds, mantras, programs or themes are unlocked through practice.
- Milestones are traditional markers (first lakh, 108 days in a row), not bronze, silver and gold badge tiers.
- **Premium stays as planned** for premium content ([partners-and-revenue](../product/features/partners-and-revenue.md)). Free flow means nothing is earned through counts or streaks; it does not change what is premium.
