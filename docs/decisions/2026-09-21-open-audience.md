# Audience: open to everyone

## Context

We considered choosing one first audience: people in India, the diaspora, or Western spiritual seekers.

## Decision

The app is an **open platform for anyone interested**, without distinguishing between groups:

- People in the Indian subcontinent — India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Bangladesh and beyond
- The Indian and Nepali diaspora — USA, Canada, UK, Australia and elsewhere
- Spiritual seekers everywhere

## Consequences

- **Languages:** multilingual from the start; which languages ship in P1 is an [open question](../product/open-questions.md).
- **Calendars and festivals:** must cover regional differences (e.g. Dashain and Tihar in Nepal, Vikram Samvat and Nepal Sambat).
- **Pricing:** regional pricing so the app is affordable everywhere.
- **Content:** articles written for both lifelong devotees and complete beginners.
- **Marketing** may still start in specific communities; the product itself stays open to all.
