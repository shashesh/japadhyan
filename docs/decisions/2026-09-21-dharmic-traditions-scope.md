# Scope: Dharmic traditions of India and Indo-Asia

## Context

Early brainstorming imagined the app for "every faith", including Islamic dhikr and the Christian rosary. But the practice this app is built around — naam japam, repeating the Name — is shared most closely by the traditions native to the Indian subcontinent and wider Indo-Asia.

## Decision

The app serves **Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist and Jain** traditions (and related traditions of the region). Islamic and Christian recitation practices are **out of scope**.

## Consequences

- Shared concepts (mala, mantra, japa/simran, sankalpa) fit naturally across all supported traditions.
- Hindu traditions launch in P1; Sikh, Buddhist and Jain in P4, each with an advisor.
- Partially supersedes the "every faith" wording in [2026-09-21-full-scope-in-phases](2026-09-21-full-scope-in-phases.md); its phasing still stands.
