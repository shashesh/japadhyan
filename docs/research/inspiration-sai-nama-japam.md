# Inspiration: the Sai app's Nama Japam screen

Source: a screen recording (2026-09-21) of the Nama Japam feature in a Sathya Sai app.

## What the screen does

- Header: image of Swami and the instruction — *"Sai Ram! Tap 'Aum Sri Sai Ram' in a sequence. For every 11 entries, offer at the Lotus feet of Swami by clicking on Offer Japam."*
- A large running **count** (e.g. 59, 61, 62, 64).
- A text field (**"Type here…"**) — you can type the mantra instead of tapping.
- Four word tiles: **AUM · SRI · SAI · RAM**. The next expected tile is highlighted with a hand pointer; completed tiles get a green tick.
- An **"Offer Japam"** button showing the lotus feet, used every 11 entries.
- Bottom navigation: Home, Watch, Listen, Read, Gallery.

## What we keep

1. **Word-by-word, in-order tapping** — keeps attention on each word instead of mindless counting. → [word-by-word tap](../product/features/chanting-modes.md#word-by-word-tap)
2. **The offering ritual** — each batch ends in an act of offering. → [offering moment](../product/features/session-experience.md#the-offering-moment)
3. **Typing as an alternative** — the seed of [likhita japa](../product/features/chanting-modes.md#likhita-japa).

## What we do differently

- Serve every deity and tradition, not one organization.
- Many chanting modes feeding one count.
- Progress, sankalpas and festival programs.
- Content sits behind chanting, not beside it.
